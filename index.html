Profile & Bio

Sir Haruna Garba Gaidam is a Registered Community Health Practitioner (RCHP) and final-year CHEW student at Alma'arif College of Nursing, Midwifery & Health Sciences. With a mission to revolutionize rural healthcare delivery, he combines clinical excellence with community-centered innovation.

Trained in emergency response, maternal health, and disease surveillance, Sir Haruna is building a legacy of impact across Northeast Nigeria — one village at a time.

To lead the digital transformation of primary healthcare in underserved regions by integrating mobile clinics, AI-driven diagnostics, and telemedicine outreach.
