Profile & Bio

I am Sir Haruna Garba Gaidam, a passionate and compassionate Community Health Extension Worker (CHEW) in training at Alma'arif College of Nursing, Midwifery & Health Sciences. With a deep commitment to improving rural healthcare access, I focus on preventive care, maternal-child health, and community education.

Certified as a Registered Community Health Practitioner (RCHP), I combine clinical skills with cultural sensitivity to serve underserved populations with dignity and excellence.

Career Goals

My mission is to bridge the healthcare gap in rural Nigeria by establishing mobile clinics and community wellness programs that prioritize maternal and child health.

I aim to lead public health initiatives that integrate digital health tools and community engagement to improve early diagnosis and disease prevention across Northern Nigeria.
